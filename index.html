Seller-declared document. It carries five of the nineteen publicly accessible categories in Annex XIII Part 1 of Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 — identification, material composition, recycled content, carbon footprint and end-of-life information. Two of those five are narrower here than the regulation asks: the carbon footprint is one figure where Article 7 requires a declaration per battery model and manufacturing plant, with the life-cycle breakdown and a link to the supporting study, and recycled content is one percentage where Article 8(1) requires the share for cobalt, lithium and nickel and the share for lead separately. It does not carry the other fourteen categories, which include rated capacity, voltage, power capability, expected lifetime, round-trip energy efficiency, internal resistance, c-rate, renewable content, the responsible-sourcing due-diligence report and the EU declaration of conformity. It is not an officially registered passport; registration and conformity assessment are separate obligations.
